How It Works

Three steps to clarity.

No account, setup, or spreadsheet expertise. Go from a downloaded report to useful decisions in moments.

STEP 01

Download Your Report

Open the Reports area in Flipkart Seller Hub and export the data you want to understand.

  • Sales or Return report
  • CSV and Excel files supported
Open Flipkart Seller Hub↗
STEP 02

Upload to SellerMetric

Drop your downloaded report into the analyzer. There is no setup, signup, or account required.

  • Drag and drop in seconds
  • Processed privately in your browser
STEP 03

Get Instant Insights

Move from raw spreadsheet rows to clear KPIs, charts, and product-level opportunities.

  • Interactive charts and tables
  • Actionable seller performance KPIs
⌾

Your data never leaves
your device.

SellerMetric runs entirely in your browser. We don’t store, upload, or transmit your Flipkart data to any server. Everything is processed locally using JavaScript—complete privacy, zero risk.
