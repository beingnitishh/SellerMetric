# SellerMetric Search Intent and Content Strategy

## Positioning boundary

SellerMetric analyzes standard Flipkart Sales and Return reports locally in the browser. It does not currently provide competitor tracking, keyword-rank tracking, fee reconciliation, settlement matching, profit calculation, advertising analysis, or official Flipkart integration. SEO content must not imply those capabilities.

## Initial competitive observations

Current search results include:

- marketplace analytics products focused on competitor price and keyword tracking
- seller-service agencies publishing return and performance advice
- reconciliation products focused on settlements and fee leakage
- generic seller-dashboard guides
- free browser-based marketplace tools

SellerMetric should compete where its product has genuine strength: transparent Sales/Return report methodology, local browser processing, SKU-level analysis, RTO/customer-return separation, and clear limitations. It should not copy claims about competitor intelligence, profit, fees, rankings, or reconciliation.

## Editorial standard

Every future guide must:

1. map to a real seller decision;
2. use report fields SellerMetric actually supports or clearly name additional required data;
3. state formulas and denominators;
4. label fictional numbers as illustrative;
5. distinguish correlation, observation, and recommended investigation;
6. link to relevant product functionality and supporting guides;
7. include an independent-tool/trademark disclosure;
8. be reviewed when Flipkart report structures or SellerMetric calculations change.
